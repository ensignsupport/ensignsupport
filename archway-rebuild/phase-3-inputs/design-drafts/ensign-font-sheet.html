Ensign: font pairings

Phase 2, Step 4b. 16 Sep 2026. Sample text uses real Ensign facts but is not final copy (copy is Phase 3). Not locked.

What every option had to pass
  • Easy to read for tired or low vision eyes: letters that are hard to confuse (I, l, 1 and O, 0), tall lowercase letters, comfortable at 18px body size
  • Warm, not clinical: Andrew asked for mild, warm and welcoming
  • Premium, not templated: no AI default fonts
  • Free and fast: on Google Fonts, self-hostable in Next.js, few files to load
Ruled out
  • Outfit and Manrope: what Anand's build used. Both sit on Taste Skill's go-to list, so they read as templated
  • Inter: the most common AI default
  • Fraunces and Instrument Serif: Taste Skill bans them as the two AI-favourite display serifs
  • Script or decorative fonts: hard to read for people with cognitive or psychosocial disability
