Ensign: Design A, more indigo

Phase 2, Step 6. 16 Sep 2026. Same layout as Design A, three strengths of Andrew's indigo. Not locked. Scroll inside each frame.

What changed
  • Only colour. Layout, fonts, facts and copy are the same as Design A.
  • Every colour comes from the indigo scale built from Andrew's pigment photo, and every text pairing used here passed the readability check on the colour sheet.
  • Recommended: A2. It makes the site clearly indigo while keeping the first screen light, calm and easy to read, which matters for tired or low vision eyes.

A1. Indigo accents

Warm stone page. Headlines, the selected option and the scribble go indigo. The facts strip sits on a soft indigo tint.

Trade-off: Closest to Therapy in London. Colour is noticeable but still quiet.
