## 1. About this policy

1.1 Ensign Support Coordination ("Ensign", "we", "us", "our") is a sole trader business owned and operated by Andrew Manoba. We provide Support Coordination and Psychosocial Recovery Coaching to people with an NDIS plan.

1.2 We provide a health service and we handle health information. This means we are bound by the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles ("APPs"), whatever the size of our business.

1.3 This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we hold, use and disclose it, and how you can see it, correct it or complain.

1.4 In this policy, "you" means anyone whose personal information we handle. This includes NDIS participants, their families, carers, nominees and guardians, and people who refer to us, such as support coordinators, Local Area Coordinators, plan managers, health professionals and other providers.

1.5 We are an unregistered NDIS provider. We still follow the NDIS Code of Conduct.

1.6 **If someone helps you make decisions.** For children, we usually deal with a parent or guardian, and we involve young people who can make their own decisions. If a participant has an NDIS nominee or a guardian, we deal with them as their authority allows. We still talk with the participant directly, in a way that works for them, and we respect their wishes wherever we can. We check that consent is still current before we share information.

1.7 **Changing your mind.** You can change your mind about consent at any time by telling us in any way that suits you. If you need support to decide, we explain things in a way that works for you and give you time. Withdrawing consent does not affect sharing that has already happened. If you withdraw consent, we might not be able to keep providing some services. We will explain what this means for you.

## 2. Key terms

2.1 **Personal information** is information or an opinion about you, or that could identify you. This is true even if it is wrong, or not written down.

2.2 **Sensitive information** includes health information and information about racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, sexual orientation and criminal record.

2.3 **Health information** includes information about a person's health or disability, the health services provided to them, and their expressed wishes about future health services.

## 3. What we collect

3.1 **About participants**, we collect:
(a) name, date of birth, address, phone number and email;
(b) NDIS number, NDIS plan, plan dates, funded supports and how the plan is managed;
(c) health and disability information, including diagnoses, functional capacity reports, allied health and occupational therapy reports, and support needs;
(d) the names and contact details of family members, carers, nominees, guardians and current providers;
(e) cultural background, preferred language and whether an interpreter is needed;
(f) notes of our meetings, calls and progress, and records of the supports we coordinate; and
(g) billing information needed to invoice a plan manager or a self managed participant.

## 4. How we collect it

4.1 Where reasonable and practical, we collect personal information directly from you. This can be in person, by phone, by email, or through the referral or enquiry forms on our website.

4.2 We also collect information from other people when you have agreed, or when it is unreasonable or impractical to collect it from you. This includes referrers, family members, nominees, guardians, plan managers, health professionals, other providers and the National Disability Insurance Agency ("NDIA").

4.3 **Referrals made for someone else.** Please ask the person, or their nominee or guardian, before you refer them. If they do not know about the referral yet, tell us on the form. Our first contact will then be to explain who we are and ask whether they want our help. We will tell them who referred them, what information we received, and that they can see or correct it. 4.4 If we receive personal information we did not ask for, we decide whether we could have collected it lawfully. If not, we destroy it or de-identify it as soon as practical, where lawful and reasonable to do so.

## 5. Why we collect it

5.1 We collect, hold, use and disclose personal information to:
(a) respond to referrals and enquiries;
(b) decide whether our services suit a participant;
(c) provide Support Coordination and Psychosocial Recovery Coaching, including finding, contacting and working with providers;
(d) prepare progress reports and plan review reports;
(e) invoice plan managers or self managed participants;
(f) keep appropriate records;
(g) respond to feedback, complaints and incidents; and
(h) meet our legal obligations.

5.2 If you do not give us the information we ask for, we might not be able to accept a referral or deliver our services.

5.3 You can phone us with a general question without giving your name, or you can use a different name. Our online forms ask for a name and phone number so we can reply. To deliver services, we need to know who you are.
