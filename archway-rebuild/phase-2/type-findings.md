# Ensign: Type findings

Phase 2, Step 6. 17 Sep 2026. Shortlist only. Final choice happens in Phase 3.

## What we learned
- Allen rejected 11 pairings tested inside an AI-built layout. The layout, not only the fonts, made them look AI generated (C20).
- Fonts measured on the picked reference and award winners show common fonts can look premium when set well: Therapy in London uses Figtree, Therapy Warsaw uses Inter, Crown Institute uses Helvetica Neue. Setting (size, weight, spacing) and restraint matter more than the name.
- Premium health and wellbeing sites often use foundry fonts: Mosh (Clearface, Maison Neue), Leap in! (Founders Grotesk, Klim), Twofold Therapy (GT America, Grilli Type), Maxima Therapy (ABC Diatype Rounded, Dinamo), Heva Health (PP Editorial New, Pangram Pangram).
- Local competitors all use common free fonts: Poppins, Lato, Quicksand, Nunito.

## Shortlist
| # | Pairing | Why | Source and licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Figtree for everything, set large at regular weight with tight spacing | Exactly what Allen's picked reference does. Rounded, friendly, readable | Google Fonts, free, commercial use allowed |
| 2 | GT America (headings and text) | Used by Twofold Therapy, a solo practice. Calm, professional, premium. Trial fonts for testing | Grilli Type, paid web licence (check current price) |
| 3 | ABC Diatype Rounded (headings) with a highly legible text face | Used by Maxima Therapy (Site of the Day). Warmer and softer | Dinamo, paid web licence (check current price) |
| 4 (body option) | Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for long reading text with any of the above | Built by the Braille Institute for low vision readers | Google Fonts, free |

## Ruled out, and why
- Outfit and Manrope: Anand's build, on Taste Skill's go-to list, read as templated.
- Inter as a default, Fraunces, Instrument Serif: AI default fonts.
- Script or decorative fonts for text (Photograph Signature, Metamorphous): hard to read for people with cognitive or psychosocial disability. A real hand-drawn signature is used instead.
- Century Gothic for body text: letters easy to confuse (I and l), tiring for long reading.
