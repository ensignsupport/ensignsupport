Rules this sets
  • Light mode: warm off-white page, near-black warm text, indigo-600 for buttons and links, indigo-900 for headings and dark bands.
  • Dark mode: deep indigo-950 page, indigo-50 text, lighter indigo-300 for buttons and links.
  • The one fail is on purpose: the pigment indigo itself cannot be used as text in dark mode (2.77:1). In dark mode it only appears as a background or decoration.
  • Every pair that will ship passes, most by a wide margin (5.1:1 to 17:1). Good for readers with low vision or cognitive fatigue.
