Ensign: reference board

Phase 2, direction step. 16 Sep 2026. 18 live sites made by real studios, all recognised by Awwwards, in therapy, mental health, care and health. Fonts measured on each live site.

What to do
  • Scroll the board. Open the live sites that catch your eye (the screenshot is just the award thumbnail).
  • Pick 3 to 5 by number.
  • For each, write a few words on what you like: the fonts, the layout, the colour, the photos, the feeling, the way information is laid out.
  • Add any other sites you love, from any industry.
  • Ignore that some are for other countries or services. We take the design moves, not the content.
One thing the fonts already show
  • Several award winners use very common fonts: Inter, Helvetica, Figtree, Roboto, even Fraunces.
  • So what makes them feel designed is mostly layout, space, photography, colour and restraint, not the font name alone.
  • That is why our font test inside one template could never feel right. Your picks here will tell us the whole direction, and the fonts come with it.
