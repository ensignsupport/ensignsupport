# Ensign: Design and content parameters

Phase 2, Step 3. 17 Sep 2026. Every design and word in Phase 3 must fit inside these. Status: DRAFT (confirm at Phase 2 lock).

## Audience needs (source: research-audiences.md)
- Answer practical questions first: what support coordination and recovery coaching are, who it is for, area, plan types, who you will speak to, how fast they reply, how to refer.
- Trust comes from a real named person you will actually speak to, independence, clear limits, and replying.
- Low reading load: plain words, short paths, visuals over text walls (W3C COGA; Australian Style Manual Easy Read guidance).
- No pressure or alarm: calm tone and imagery (trauma-informed findings, Every Australian Counts).
- Referrers need a fast lane: area, plan types, capacity to take referrals, referral form.

## Client's fixed choices (source: facts.md)
- Colour: indigo, light to dark, light and dark mode. Mild, warm and welcoming.
- Pages: Home, About, Services, Referral, Contact.
- Andrew's real photo. No testimonials or numbers exist. Never invent.
- Unregistered status stated in the footer only.
- Referral form collects health information (Andrew consents), so the privacy policy and collection notice are required.
- No logo yet. Logo is designed in Phase 3.

## Hard rules
- WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard access. Reduced motion respected.
- Facts only from facts.md. Placeholders tagged and signed off before go live.
- Archway copy and claims rules: pain as a scene, mechanism not outcome, no banned words, no triads, no em dashes. Client wishes win except for legal items.
- Health details never sent by text message.

## Bar to beat (source: benchmark.html, two blind rounds)
- The strongest site in both scoring rounds is Mosh (premium online health). The best NDIS sites are Hireup and Leap in!. No local competitor scores well on trust.
- Target: beat all local competitors on every criterion, and match or beat Mosh on clarity, trust and reading load.

## Style presets (Taste Skill, trust-first audience)
- Layout variance 3 to 4 (calm, not experimental). Motion 2 to 3 (small, purposeful). Density 4 to 5 (few words per screen, generous space).
- AI tells to avoid: centred hero over gradient or mesh, three equal feature cards, pill tags everywhere, four-stat strips, eyebrow line plus big headline plus two buttons as the default hero, particle or mesh backgrounds, Inter by default.

## Direction principles (source: reference-analysis-therapyin-london.md, Allen's words)
- Precise, restrained layout plus one hand-made human mark per screen ("mild perfect imperfection").
- Big type at regular weight, deliberate line breaks, lots of space, one accent colour.
- Task first: the visitor does something simple instead of reading a pitch.
- Conversational enquiry that does not feel like a form (Archway house pattern, C23).
- Visuals over text for processes and key facts (C30).
- Real hand-made assets only: Andrew's signature, scribbles, painted brush strokes.
