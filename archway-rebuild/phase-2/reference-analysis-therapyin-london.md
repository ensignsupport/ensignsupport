Picked by Allen, 16 Sep 2026. Measured live at https://www.therapyin.london/ (desktop, 1024px wide).
Awwwards Honorable Mention 2025.

## Measured design moves

| Area | What they do | Measured |
|---|---|---|
| Font | One family only, used for everything | Figtree (free, Google Fonts) |
| Headline weight | Regular, not bold | 400 |
| Headline size | Huge brand line | 128px, letter spacing -5px (about -4%) |

| Background | Warm stone, not white, not cream | #EDEAE1 |
| Text | Soft near-black, not pure black | #2A2A2A |
| Accent | One colour only, a clear blue | #3881F1 |
| Corners | Soft, generous | 30px on buttons and cards |
| Words | Very few | about 430 words over 12 screens |
| First screen | A task, not a sales pitch: "What do you need help with?" and "Where are you based?" then Find a Therapist | |
| Structure | Task first, brand line, short promise, articles by named therapists, 3 steps, press logos, therapist sign-up | |
| People | Real named therapists, personal profiles ("taste in films") | |

## Why it feels designed, not AI made
- Restraint: 1 font, 1 background, 1 text colour, 1 accent.
- Big type set at regular weight with tight spacing. AI defaults to bold, medium-size headings.
- Huge amounts of space. Very few words per screen.
- The first thing you do is a task that matches the visitor's question.

## What it means for Ensign
- The font name is not the magic. Figtree is common. The setting (size, weight, spacing) and restraint are.
- Our colour direction already fits: warm stone background, soft ink text, one strong accent (indigo instead of blue).
- Task-first first screen maps well: "Who is this for?" (me, someone I support, I am a referrer) and "Where are you?" then one clear action.
- Real person, personal detail: Andrew's profile can carry the human proof, like their therapist profiles.
- Low word count directly answers Allen's complaint about wordy, tiring pages.
