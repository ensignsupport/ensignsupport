# Ensign: trust plan (no public reviews yet)

17 Sep 2026. People trust a business when they have enough of three things: **relationship**, **low risk** and **proof** (Allen's framework). Ensign has no public reviews yet, so the site leans on relationship and low risk now, and builds proof from day one. Nothing here is invented: every line is a confirmed fact or a request to Andrew.

## 1. Relationship (they feel they know Andrew)
- In the site now: Andrew's face on the first screen and in Meet Andrew; first person "Hi, I'm Andrew" on About; "One coordinator you know by name"; he comes to you in person; English and Tamil.
- Ask Andrew: a 30 to 60 second phone video or voice note ("who I am, what the first chat is like"). Shown on Home and About.
- Ask Andrew: his signature (planned mark).

## 2. Low risk (starting feels safe)
- In the site now: replies within 1 business day; actual time billed with itemised invoices; written summary before each plan review; three quick questions, no long form; referrers get a reference number and an email copy.
- Ask Andrew (confirm before using, not yet a fact): is the first chat free with no obligation? Can a participant stop at any time under his service agreement, and what notice applies?
- Clear limits stated up front (NDIA managed plans, not a crisis service) so nobody is misled.

## 3. Proof (he can deliver)
- In the site now: 7+ years in disability and mental health; Bachelor and Master of Social Work (UQ); Worker Screening, Blue Card, Police Check; past roles (support coordinator, social worker, disability clinician); a trust strip under the first screen.
- Ask Andrew: 1 or 2 genuine, written-permission quotes from participants, families or referrers he already works with (first name and role only, or role only). Never invented, never edited to change meaning.
- Ask Andrew: professional endorsements (for example a LinkedIn recommendation from a colleague) he is happy to quote.
- Ask Andrew: an anonymised sample of his written plan review summary, to show the work itself.
- Build proof from launch (Phase 7 hypercare): set up a Google Business Profile; after a good first month, Andrew asks each participant or family if they would leave a review, with a simple link; add real reviews to the site when there are at least 3.

## Design rule
- Until real proof exists, never show empty review sections, star ratings or placeholder quotes on the live site.
