Lines you can draw with a pen

Quick direction sketches, not finished art. Each is a simple gesture you could draw on paper in a few seconds, in one colour, at a steady thickness. Pick a direction (or two) and I will refine it and run the blind check. Small strip under each: header sizes.

H1 · Running stitch
Like a hand-sewn seam. Stitches pieces together. Draw it: short dashes.

H2 · Kolam
From Tamil kolam, where one hand-drawn line loops around dots. Here it circles three dots: you, your supports, Andrew. Draw it: three dots, one line with three loops.

H3 · One loop under the akku
A single loop right under the akku, like tying things together. Draw it: a line with one loop.

H4 · Dots become a line
Starts as scattered dots, becomes one clear line. Unclear to clear. Draw it: dots, then a line.

H5 · Little hops
Four small arches, one step at a time. Draw it: a row of bumps.

H6 · Starts with a spiral
Starts where you are, then heads out straight. Draw it: a small curl, then a line.
