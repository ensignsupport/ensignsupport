- Colours: wordmark #231f19 (dark #faf6f1), akku #2850d3 (dark #a0bdfe), stroke #6993fa (dark #a0bdfe).
- Icon: akku rings on a warm tile (#faf6f1) or deep tile (#0f225e). All three rings open, matching the real letter (blind check: a filled top ring looked like the Asana logo or a figure).
