  • Every gap on the page uses one spacing step. Nothing in between.
  • Sections are separated by 64px on phone, up to 128px on a large screen.
  • One idea per section. At most one heading size and one text size per section.
  • Text lines stop at 60 characters wide, so eyes don't get lost.
  • Body text is 18px, bigger than most sites, for easier reading.
  • Everything tappable is at least 48px tall.
  • Indigo is for actions and marks only. The page stays warm and calm.
  • Motion: presses shrink slightly (160ms). Nothing slides or bounces. Turned off for people who ask for less motion.
  • Both fonts are free for commercial web use (SIL Open Font License).
